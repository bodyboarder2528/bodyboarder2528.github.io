Privacy policy

Hermes is a personal app used only by its owner, Alan Jeff. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Hermes reads the owner's own Google account data (such as Gmail messages and calendar events) to sort email and show reminders to the owner.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to provide these features to the owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data stays on the owner's own computers, except where it is processed by the services the owner has chosen to use.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the owner's Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps with account access.
